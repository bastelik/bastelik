# Evaluator Checklist

Use this checklist for each evaluation. Keep a record of evidence rather than relying on impressions.

## A. Understand the task
- [ ] Identify the user's actual objective.
- [ ] Extract explicit instructions, constraints, requested format, and audience.
- [ ] Identify domain-specific or time-sensitive claims.
- [ ] Note what information or sources are available and what is missing.

## B. Decompose the response
- [ ] Separate the answer into material claims, recommendations, and actions.
- [ ] Preserve dates, quantities, names, qualifiers, and conditions.
- [ ] Identify assumptions and inferences.
- [ ] Flag claims that require external verification.

## C. Verify evidence
- [ ] Use sources appropriate to the claim and domain.
- [ ] Prefer primary or authoritative sources where available.
- [ ] Check whether the cited source actually supports the specific claim.
- [ ] Record conflicts between sources and explain the hierarchy used.
- [ ] Classify each material claim as supported, contradicted, or not established.
- [ ] Do not interpret missing evidence as proof that a claim is false.

## D. Assess response quality
- [ ] Score factual accuracy.
- [ ] Score evidence and source handling.
- [ ] Score instruction adherence.
- [ ] Score completeness and relevance.
- [ ] Score consistency and coherence.
- [ ] Score language and communication.
- [ ] Score safety and sensitivity where applicable.

## E. Decide and document
- [ ] Check for critical failures before calculating the overall result.
- [ ] Calculate the mean of applicable scores.
- [ ] Record concrete examples supporting each low score.
- [ ] State the required correction in actionable terms.
- [ ] Identify whether re-review is required.

## F. Calibration and consistency
- [ ] Compare borderline cases with previously documented precedents.
- [ ] Apply canonical terminology and scoring definitions consistently.
- [ ] Escalate ambiguous policy or domain questions rather than inventing a rule.
- [ ] Record new precedents so future evaluators can apply them consistently.
