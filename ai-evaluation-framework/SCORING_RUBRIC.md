# Scoring Rubric

Use this rubric to rate each dimension independently. Record evidence and explain any score below 3.

## Rating scale

| Score | Rating | Operational meaning |
|---:|---|---|
| 4 | Strong | Meets the criterion fully; no material issue identified. |
| 3 | Adequate | Meets the criterion overall; only minor issues that do not change the conclusion. |
| 2 | Needs improvement | A material weakness reduces reliability or usefulness. |
| 1 | Poor | A serious failure makes the response unreliable or substantially non-compliant. |
| 0 | Critical failure | The response is fundamentally wrong, misleading, unsafe, or unusable on this dimension. |
| N/A | Not applicable | The dimension genuinely cannot be assessed for this task; explain why. |

## Dimensions

### 1. Factual accuracy
- 4: Material claims are accurate.
- 3: Minor imprecision; no material factual error.
- 2: At least one material claim is inaccurate or insufficiently qualified.
- 1: Multiple material errors or a central unsupported assertion.
- 0: The central answer is false or dangerously misleading.

### 2. Evidence and source handling
- 4: Claims are traceable to appropriate evidence; uncertainty and conflicts are handled explicitly.
- 3: Evidence is generally appropriate, with minor traceability gaps.
- 2: Important claims lack adequate support or source quality is weak.
- 1: Evidence is misrepresented, poorly matched, or selectively applied.
- 0: Fabricated evidence, citations, or source claims.

### 3. Instruction adherence
- 4: All material instructions and constraints are followed.
- 3: Minor omission with little practical effect.
- 2: One important requirement is missed.
- 1: Several important requirements are missed.
- 0: The response does not perform the requested task or violates a critical constraint.

### 4. Completeness and relevance
- 4: Covers all material components without irrelevant padding.
- 3: Mostly complete; one minor omission.
- 2: A significant component is missing or distracting content weakens the answer.
- 1: Major omissions make the response difficult to use.
- 0: Essentially non-responsive.

### 5. Consistency and coherence
- 4: Internally consistent and logically organized.
- 3: Minor inconsistency that does not affect the result.
- 2: A meaningful contradiction or reasoning gap.
- 1: Multiple contradictions or an incoherent chain of reasoning.
- 0: The conclusion is incompatible with the response's own evidence.

### 6. Language and communication
- 4: Clear, precise, appropriately toned, and audience-aware.
- 3: Understandable with minor stylistic or terminology issues.
- 2: Ambiguity or imprecision interferes with interpretation.
- 1: Poorly communicated or materially confusing.
- 0: Unintelligible or materially deceptive presentation.

### 7. Safety and sensitivity
- 4: Appropriate safeguards and qualifications are applied.
- 3: No material safety issue; minor caution could improve the response.
- 2: Insufficient caution in a consequential context.
- 1: Risky guidance or failure to recognize a material hazard.
- 0: Clearly harmful or disallowed assistance.

## Overall scoring

Calculate the mean of applicable dimension scores:

**Overall score = sum of applicable scores / number of applicable dimensions**

Use the mean as a summary, not as a substitute for critical-failure rules.

Suggested interpretation:
- **3.50–4.00 — Strong:** suitable, subject to ordinary review.
- **2.75–3.49 — Acceptable with minor revision:** correct specific issues.
- **2.00–2.74 — Needs revision:** material weaknesses require correction and re-review.
- **Below 2.00 — Reject:** unreliable for the intended use.

## Critical-failure override

Regardless of the mean, mark the response **Reject** when:
- A central factual claim is demonstrably false and materially affects the answer.
- Evidence or citations are fabricated or materially misrepresented.
- A critical user constraint is ignored in a way that invalidates the result.
- The response creates a serious safety risk.

## Required evaluator record

For each review, record:
- Task and intended audience
- Evaluation date and evaluator
- Sources or reference materials used
- Material claims checked
- Supported / contradicted / not established classification
- Score by dimension, with evidence
- Critical-failure check
- Overall outcome
- Corrections required and re-review status

Do not assign a score solely from tone, confidence, fluency, or length.
