## Status definitions

- **Green:** objectives and milestones remain achievable within agreed tolerances; no unresolved material issue requires intervention.
- **Amber:** delivery is at risk or a tolerance may be exceeded; a mitigation or decision is required.
- **Red:** an objective, milestone, budget or critical requirement is already outside tolerance or is unlikely to be achieved without intervention.

Agree thresholds with the sponsor. Never use color alone: include the rationale and the action required.
