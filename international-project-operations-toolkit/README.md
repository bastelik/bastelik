# International Project Management & Operations Toolkit

A practical set of templates and methods for planning, governing and delivering projects across organizations, countries, disciplines and distributed teams.

The toolkit is intended for project and program managers, operations leads, consultants and cross-functional teams. It can be adapted to institutional cooperation, research, language services, technology delivery, education and nonprofit programs.

## Operating cycle

1. **Frame:** establish the problem, intended outcomes, scope, constraints and decision authority.
2. **Mobilize:** identify stakeholders, workstreams, resources, dependencies and governance.
3. **Plan:** define deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria, risks and communication cadence.
4. **Deliver:** coordinate owners, monitor dependencies, manage changes and resolve blockers.
5. **Assure:** check quality and evidence of completion against agreed acceptance criteria.
6. **Close and learn:** confirm handover, document outcomes, capture lessons and assign follow-up actions.

## Core management principles

- Define outcomes and acceptance criteria before tracking activity.
- Make ownership and decision rights explicit.
- Maintain one current source of truth for plans, risks, decisions and actions.
- Escalate early, with evidence and a proposed decision.
- Distinguish a schedule variance from a delivery or outcome risk.
- Adapt governance to project complexity and risk; avoid process for its own sake.
- Treat communication as an operating mechanism, not an afterthought.

## Practical use

Copy the templates into the project workspace and tailor fields to the sponsor, delivery model, team size, reporting obligations and risk profile. The examples are generic; no client or institutional confidential information is included.
